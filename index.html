Why Resourcefulness Matters

The Resource360 Framework

Six steps toward a more resourceful household.

We look beyond income alone and consider the complete household economic picture.

01 — UNDERSTAND

Know your money

Understand income, expenses, savings, obligations and financial priorities.

02 — OPTIMIZE

Reduce leakage

Identify avoidable costs and improve the efficiency of household spending.

03 — UTILIZE

Use what you have

Identify underused assets, skills, time, knowledge and networks.

04 — CREATE

Create income

Develop practical additional revenue streams suited to the household.

05 — INVEST

Put resources to work

Learn how productive investments can support long-term financial goals.

06 — GROW

Build resilience

Turn better systems and productive resources into sustainable financial progress.
