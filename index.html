Household Financial Baseline

Know what your money must do before deciding what it can do.

This is not an ordinary expense tracker. Resource360 separates resources, operating needs, commitments, predictable future costs and protected reserves so your household can see its true financial capacity.

Reliable ResourcesAdjust income for frequency and reliability instead of treating every rupee equally.
True SurplusRecognize future known expenses before calling remaining cash a surplus.
Financial RunwayMeasure how long resilience reserves could support essential household operations.
